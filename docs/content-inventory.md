## 4. Notes & gaps

1. **FAQ answers are mostly missing.** `/faq` uses accordions; only the expanded answer is
   present in the DOM. Five stakeholder tabs exist (Investor, Industry, Startup, Student,
   Researcher) and each holds several Q&As. The full set needs exporting from the source
   or the CMS.
2. **`/tenders` is a placeholder.** It states notices "will be published here as issued"
   and links to the Government Orders library. No tender content exists yet.
3. **`/contact` is a form, not a content page.** One intake with an Intent selector:
   invest · pilot · build (startup) · establish (manufacturer) · research · learn ·
   testbed · site visit · media · bulk-cloud · use-case.
4. **Quantum Security has no route.** It is listed in the Missions menu marked
   "Coming soon".
5. **Tab and carousel content is partially captured.** Pages using tabbed panels
   (`/ecosystem`, `/faq`, `/dashboard`) only render the active tab, so inactive panels are
   not in this transcription.
6. **Numbers to keep consistent site-wide** — these recur and must not drift:
   3.98 K · ~1.5 lakh learners · 3,000 advanced cohort · 105 pipeline / 15 operational ·
   365 hrs/yr cloud runtime · 24 quantum + 98 AI use cases · ~14% faster (69 → 60 min) ·
   85 → 100% localisation · 10 companies / 75 people at Medha Towers · 20,000+ hackathon
   participants · ₹1,000 crore Quantum Fund (G.O.Ms.No.23) · GO Ms.No.54 (incentives) ·
   Declaration 7 Jul 2025 · export licence 18 Jun 2026 · Amaravati 1Q launch 14 Apr 2026.
7. **Every claim on the live site carries a status and a source** — DELIVERED / LIVE /
   IN PROGRESS / OPEN, plus a date and "Source: AQV". That convention is the spine of the
   content and should survive any redesign.
