Amaravati Quantum Valley · Design direction · Sheet A

Drawn like the city it is actually building.

Amaravati is a purpose-built capital. So the site borrows the language that already belongs to it — architectural survey: ink linework on drafting paper, measured annotation, orthographic plans, and one inked indigo. The Declaration ledger becomes a drawing revision register, because that is genuinely what it is: promises, status, evidence, date.
