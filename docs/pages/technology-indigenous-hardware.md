01 — THE STORY


### From 85% localization to open hardware.


- AUG 2025 Opportunity identified Build a comprehensive indigenous quantum hardware ecosystem from Amaravati.
- 9 SEP 2025 The turning point Scientists and startups meet the Hon’ble Chief Minister; conclusion: ~85% of components can be built in India. CM directive: build quantum computers from Amaravati — “Made in Amaravati” for the world.
- 14 APR 2026 Amaravati 1Q launches Launch of quantum reference facilities — Medha Towers (by Qubitech) and SRM University-AP campus, supported by Qbit Force. Amaravati 1Q: an open quantum computer — built across India, made in Amaravati.
