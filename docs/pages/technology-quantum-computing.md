Dilution refrigeration stack that holds the processor at millikelvin operating temperatures.


#### Quantum processor

Superconducting qubit chip — the computational core of IBM Quantum System Two.


#### Control electronics

Room-temperature and cryogenic control hardware that drives, reads and stabilises qubits.
