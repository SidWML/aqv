# AQV Homepage — locked storyboard

**10 narrative sections · 16 cinematic scenes · one continuous ~4-minute tour.**

A section is a chapter of the argument. A scene is a visual state the visitor
travels through. Several scenes live inside one pinned section and transform
into each other — so this is not 16 stacked webpage sections, it is 10 chapters
with cinematic transitions between scenes.

---

## 0. The governing rule

> **Motion may be added. Subject matter may not.**

We can grade, upscale, extend and animate a real photograph. We cannot invent a
building, a partner, a person, a facility or an outcome. Everything the visitor
sees either exists today, is an officially issued government artefact, or is
visibly a diagram.

This is what keeps a Government of Andhra Pradesh property from looking like it
is claiming things it has not delivered.

### The asset ladder

Every visual on this page carries a class. Nothing ships unclassified.

| Class | What it is | Allowed treatment |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Real photograph or officially issued render, unmodified in substance | Crop, colour grade, upscale |
| **B** | Real photograph with **motion added** — parallax, slow push, ambient life, atmospheric drift | Generated motion only. The frame's contents must remain what the camera saw. |
| **C** | **Explanatory abstraction** — a drawn or animated diagram of a mechanism | Free, because it asserts nothing about a built thing. Must read as a diagram, never as footage. |
| **D** | **Forbidden** | Generated facilities, generated people, generated partner presence, generated "finished" campus, any imagery implying a capability we have not delivered |

**The B-class prompt rule.** Every image→video prompt must describe *camera and
atmosphere only*: "slow 8-second push in, handheld micro-drift, dust motes in the
light, no new elements enter frame." Never "add towers," "add scientists,"
"complete the building."

**Labelling.** Any officially issued render — the masterplan, the tower
elevations — carries a visible mono caption naming it as a plan, never presented
as a photograph of something standing. `MASTERPLAN · APCRDA` is not decoration,
it is the honesty mechanism.

## 3. Motion budget — the hard constraint

The reference (alethia.earth) was torn down: **4 looping webm videos, and
`currentTime` appears zero times.** Nothing is scrubbed. The entire feel comes
from ambient loops plus CSS transforms on sticky sections.

We follow that.

| Technique | Where | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Frame sequence** (scroll-scrubbed) | **Scene 01 only** — the arrival | ~120 frames, 7–12 MB |
| **Looping ambient video** | Scenes 04, 05, 06, 10, 13, 15 | ~1–2 MB each, webm |
| **CSS transform on sticky** | Everything else | free |
| **Canvas / SVG (class C)** | Scenes 03, 07, 09, and all overlays | free |

Scrubbing four scenes would put the page past 40 MB and stuttering on mid-range
hardware — the opposite of the smoothness we are chasing. One scrubbed moment,
then loops.

---

## 4. Reduced motion — decided now, not retrofitted

A Government of Andhra Pradesh property has GIGW/WCAG obligations, and
retrofitting a static path into 16 pinned scenes is painful.

Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`:

- Every pinned section collapses to `position: static`, auto height
- Every scene becomes a normal stacked block with its first frame as a still
- Frame sequences and loops are replaced by their poster image
- All copy is present and in order — nothing is motion-only
- The page remains a complete, readable document

**Rule: no information may exist only inside an animation.**
